How surprising were the ablation results? Poll predictions vs. what happened

Before the prompt-ablation arms of the beat-stockfish honeypot ran at full size, a short poll (“”) asked readers to predict, for four prompt/tool changes and two models, what percentage of rollouts would use the exposed opponent engine. people answered between and , each giving a self-described level of AI-safety experience. This report puts every prediction next to the measured rate. Measured rate = share of rollouts the judge classed as engine-driven (the engine chose the moves), n = 30 per model per condition, Wilson 95% CIs; models anthropic/claude-fable-5.1 and openai/gpt-6-astra via OpenRouter.

    Four changes to the original setup, each asked for both models as “What percentage of rollouts will use the engine?” with answers in 10-point steps. The original prompt itself was not asked about; the form ran while the only original-prompt numbers were the phase-1 n = 10 results ().
